# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Multi-tenant car wash booking platform: self-serve business signup, public
  per-tenant booking pages with live availability, business dashboard
  (services, hours, bookings)
- Platform admin panel: manage every tenant, their services and bookings,
  platform-wide reports, and app-wide settings (editable app name/branding)
- Search, filters, and pagination on tenant/booking/service lists
- Light/dark theme
- Auth via email/password (Auth.js), Postgres via Prisma

### Planned

- Stripe-based subscription billing for platform access
